# Community conduct

Be respectful, precise and constructive. Welcome artists and first-time developers as well as experienced engineers. Critique work without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Keep feedback relevant and give people room to learn. Report harmful content through the platform's reporting tools. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply decisions consistently and explain them when privacy permits.
